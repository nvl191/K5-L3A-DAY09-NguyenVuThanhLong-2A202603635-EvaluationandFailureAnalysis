## Limits and when to refresh

- MockLLM is deterministic and does not represent the variability or behavior of a real LLM.
- Fourteen items give limited statistical power; the gate is designed to catch clear regressions, and confidence intervals may be wide.
- Judge calibration uses public replay CSVs; independent held-out human labels are still needed.
- A defect that appears in only 1 of 3 seeds cannot be detected reliably with 5 seeds. Increase sample size based on measured noise rather than weakening the gate.
- When adding trace-derived cases or changing the evaluation population, increment the eval-set version, regenerate labels/items through scripts, and create a new baseline. When changing metrics or thresholds, increment metric-spec version and document why.

## Advanced v2 — M6 stability and A/A

- **A/A comparison:** same `swarm` on spec seeds `11/22/33/44/55` and alternate seeds `66/77/88/99/110`. Every comparable metric had Δ `0.0000` under deterministic MockLLM, including `pass_hat_5` (0.8929 on both sets).
- **Threshold:** keep blocking floor `pass_hat_5 ≥ 0.80`. It is 0.0929 below the observed baseline and outside the observed A/A noise (zero); it also satisfies the rubric's minimum floor. A real, stochastic LLM needs a new A/A measurement before carrying this threshold over.
- **Flaky variant:** run `E-flaky_regression-20261117-b1f7` had `pass_rate=0.9143`, above its simple floor 0.85, while `pass_hat_5=0.5714`, below 0.80. Its paired `pass_rate` CI also fell below zero on this 28-item set (Δ −0.064, CI [−0.114, −0.014]); therefore this run demonstrates that `pass_hat_5` catches the instability, but does not prove it was the only blocking signal.
- **Scorecard:** `lab9.mutation` blocked all 3 regressions (`seeded_regression`, `slice_regression`, `flaky_regression`) and allowed both `fixed` and `honest_refactor` (3/3, 0 false alarms).
- **Sample-size limit:** with only 5 seeds, a bug appearing in 1 of 3 seeds is not reliably detectable. Do not lower the threshold just to make that rare variant fail; increase seed count and measure the resulting noise instead.
